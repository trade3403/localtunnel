| Runner | 原生 CLI | Rathole v0.5.0 上游候选 |
|---|---|---|

| ubuntu-24.04-arm | Linux ARM64 | aarch64-unknown-linux-musl |
| macos-15-intel | macOS Intel | x86_64-apple-darwin |
